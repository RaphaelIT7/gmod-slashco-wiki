<convar name="slashco_blockbinds">
    <description>
        If enabled, GMod key binds that overlap with SlashCo's binds will be blocked from executing.<br>
        It will **NOT** attempt to block any [Blocked ConCommands](https://wiki.facepunch.com/gmod/Blocked_ConCommands), not block any bindings starting with `+` and not block any `impulse` bindings<br>
        This ConVar exists to avoid issues where a SlashCo bind may overlap with a client's GMod binding.<br>
    </description>
    <value>1</value>
    <realm>Client</realm>
</convar>
